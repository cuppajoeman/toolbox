Retopologizing a Sculpted Hand

This is a practical way to retopologize a hand sculpt before animation or game export. The goal is a clean, even, low-poly flow that keeps the silhouette and hand articulation without losing shape.

Reference workflow

Use this RetopoFlow-style flow for sculpted hands:

  1. Duplicate the hand mesh first and keep the original sculpt untouched.
  2. Keep your sculpt as the visual target.
    • Use the original sculpted hand as reference, not as the mesh you animate.
    • Turn on visibility options that make vertex and face counts easy to inspect.
  3. Set the sculpt as the shrink-wrap target.
    • For a hard-surface style: keep edge flow less curved and focus on joint ring control.
    • For a more organic style: keep denser density around knuckles, fingertips, and thumb webbing.
  4. Work broad to fine:
    • Block the whole hand with even quads.
    • Add loops at the wrist, knuckle creases, and finger bases.
    • Resolve each finger separately, keeping loop consistency from base to tip.
  5. Mirror and symmetry check: ensure both hands behave as intended, then freeze edge direction.
  6. Run topology cleanup:
    • Check for long thin triangles and flipped normals.
    • Remove degenerate poles in hard-constraint zones and reduce extra poles near joints.
  7. Export one clean hand and one test pose version before moving to armature binding.

Quality checks

  • Deform the hand through open/closed positions and confirm no large volume loss around knuckles.
  • Check edge flow with viewport smooth-shade + wire overlays.
  • Confirm each finger has a predictable loop sequence from tip to metacarpal.
